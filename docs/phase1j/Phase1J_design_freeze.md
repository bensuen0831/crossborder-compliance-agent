## Authority and compatibility

The mandatory dependency order is Applicability → Obligation → Candidate Path → Risk → Recommendation → Final Path. Each persisted stage references its immutable upstream result IDs. Risk cannot change applicability, obligations or candidate legal viability. Capability availability cannot create obligations. LLM output cannot supply any authoritative decision, score, eligibility, ranking or path; later explanation must reference formal results.

If a legal condition requires a prior assessment or statutory metric, it must be an authorized validated upstream fact/evidence reference under its legal policy. It cannot consume this J Risk stage's future output or create a cycle. Absent required assessment support remains UNKNOWN/review; ranking risk is never substituted for the statutory legal condition.

Existing `domain/contracts.py` already declares ComplianceObligationDTO, CandidateCompliancePathDTO, RiskAssessmentDTO, ComplianceRecommendationDTO and FinalCompliancePathDTO. These are incomplete transport declarations, not implemented decision authorities. Freeze a **version2 typed extension of those existing contract kinds**, within their owning domain, using the shared envelope below. Retain version1 wire schemas and meanings; version1 projections are explicitly insufficient to execute J. Never infer absent authority from their default fields. No second obligation/risk/path authority or alternate Stage1 result store is permitted. Existing classification and applicability contracts stay unchanged.

Every stage has one scoped, typed result envelope containing zero or more existing-kind v2 records. Envelope UUID is the workflow's single result_ref; item UUIDs address decisions within it. No copying of Evidence, LegalBasis, context, classification or applicability records. Domain values use stable ASCII codes; display metadata uses existing LocalizedDisplayMetadata and governed requested→fallback→canonical-name/code resolution. Locale does not participate in decision fingerprints; original source language/text/citation remains authoritative.

## Frozen stage semantics

1. **Obligation.** Consume authorized I applicability, existing H RuleHits, validated E facts and G evidence. A published OBLIGATION_POLICY binds pinned applicability config/rule identities and existing LegalBasis to typed obligation codes and conditions. Use existing `rule_ast.parse_ast/evaluate/typed_value` for closed predicates; missing facts remain unresolved. Do not add a second evaluator/DSL or change H's CLASSIFY action contract. Legal effect is a typed J policy binding, not an invented classification action. Each obligation carries the policy entry UUID, evidence and fact support for every condition.
2. **Candidate.** Instantiate published COMPLIANCE_PATH_POLICY templates and evaluate prerequisites against obligations, authorized facts/evidence and pinned capability availability. Preserve positive requirements, legal prohibitions, unmet conditions and capability gaps separately. A legally permissible template can remain unavailable because a capability is not configured; this is not a legal prohibition. No country branch or country-specific legal Python mechanism. Obligations and prerequisites identify canonical policy entries, not a new requirement catalog.
3. **Risk.** A pure deterministic function consumes only typed, authorized inputs supplied by formal application services and a pinned RISK_POLICY. No DB, vector search, LLM, agent or provider SDK imports/calls. Factors retain typed observed values, fact/evidence refs and sufficiency; dimensions and aggregate policy outputs are structured. No score for missing required inputs. Optional factors can be excluded only by explicit policy with recorded coverage; never substitute zero for missing input.
4. **Recommendation.** Compare candidate legal viability first. Rank only legally viable candidates under pinned RECOMMENDATION_POLICY and comparable risk results. Conditional paths appear as conditional alternatives, not executable selections. Risk-based escalation can require review or decline to recommend, but cannot relabel legal viability. Recommendation references immutable risk and candidate results, not recomputed LLM rankings.
5. **Final path.** Preserve selection (nullable), obligations, conditions/actions, actual prerequisites, residual risk, alternatives, all legal/evidence refs, unresolved states and complete pins. Final construction cannot turn a proposed or reviewed plan into proof that its actions have been performed. No selected path when evidence/conflict/tie prevents selection. A conditional proposal retains every unmet condition and is never APPROVED automatically.

## Status and review algebra

Store independent `input_sufficiency`, `conflict_state`, `review_required` and stable reason codes. A display/summary status cannot erase any dimension. Summary precedence: CONFLICTED → INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE → REVIEW_REQUIRED → the stage-specific ordinary status. Preserve stage-specific legal facts and a partial record set even when summary is blocked; no authoritative ready selection may use unresolved dependencies. Irrelevant NOT_APPLICABLE records do not create obligations. CONDITIONAL applicability yields conditional obligations until legally material conditions are proven. Missing/expired/unpublished policies, unknown required facts or incomplete required jurisdiction coverage produce unresolved outcomes, never an empty successful decision set.

An obligation's legal effect is REQUIRED / CONDITIONAL / NOT_APPLICABLE / UNDETERMINED; fulfillment is separately SATISFIED / UNMET / UNKNOWN / NOT_APPLICABLE. Candidate legal viability is VIABLE / CONDITIONAL / LEGALLY_PROHIBITED / UNDETERMINED; operational availability is AVAILABLE / CAPABILITY_NOT_CONFIGURED / UNAVAILABLE / UNKNOWN. A prohibition needs matched formal legal policy, canonical legal-basis and sufficient evidence; high/critical risk alone can never produce TRANSFER_NOT_ALLOWED_OR_LOCALIZATION_REQUIRED. Transfer prohibition and localization requirement are separate obligation codes, each with its own legal support; neither is a risk band.

For ranking: exclude prohibited/undetermined candidates, require comparable scoped risk outputs, then apply the published policy's ordered criteria (direction and missing-value handling explicit). Exact ties yield TIED / REVIEW_REQUIRED with all tied alternatives and no selection. An explicit independently reviewed tie-break criterion may resolve a tie; UUID/list order/locale never does. Stable-code sorting only canonicalizes serialization. Contradictory obligations/policies remain CONFLICTED; priority alone does not silently cancel a legal obligation.

Risk score mode uses decimal arithmetic: configured factor maps produce values0–100; nonnegative weights sum exactly1 per aggregation; weighted sums use the policy's explicit decimal scale and rounding. Validate nonoverlapping, exhaustive bands (lower-inclusive/upper-exclusive except final upper-inclusive). Band-only mode uses an explicit finite ordered decision table. Unknown inputs produce UNKNOWN with no numeric score. Policy must declare candidate/context scope and input coverage; outputs with different scope/policy cannot be compared by accident. RiskLevel's existing LOW/MEDIUM/HIGH/CRITICAL/UNKNOWN codes are reused.

## Reproducibility and authorization

Trusted RepositoryContext authorizes tenant/project, operation and owner restrictions before resolving any ID, including read/replay. Match project version, snapshot, context version, subject (DATA_ITEM/DATA_FLOW/SCENARIO), jurisdiction, upstream identities and exact immutable pins. Scenario-only evaluation uses E validated scenario/facts and G authorized PROJECT evidence through the existing I adapter; no fabricated DataItem/ClassificationResult. FLOW in old transport DTOs maps explicitly to canonical DATA_FLOW, never by silently changing its enum.

Pin all J policies and dependencies through existing AnalysisSnapshotRegistryPin in one explicit initialization transaction before J execution; execution cannot auto-select latest versions or expand scope. Dependencies include country/scenario/capability, H rules, I config, knowledge versions, G policies/bindings and legal-basis references. Retain an engine/schema version and canonical input/pin digest; timestamps/locale/actor display labels do not alter deterministic decisions. Historical replay retains exact versions and revalidates current access/evidence scope, without republishing or rewriting snapshots. Changed facts/config require a new approved snapshot, not mutation of an old result.

## Proposed permanent rules (not applied in J-A)

Rules1–151 remain byte-identical. Propose only:

- **152:** Formal decision dependency order is applicability→obligation→candidate→risk→recommendation→final; risk and capability availability cannot create legal obligations/prohibitions or change applicability/viability.
- **153:** Authoritative J evaluation/ranking is deterministic, governed and pinned; LLM may explain formal results only and cannot decide obligations, viability, scores, recommendations or paths.
- **154:** Final/recommendation results cannot conceal conflict, insufficiency, review or unmet legal conditions; unresolved selection remains absent and formal legal prohibition requires traceable legal authority.
- **155:** J results retain canonical upstream/legal/evidence identities, immutable snapshot/config/policy provenance and idempotent authorization-revalidated persistence; workflow checkpoint/locale is not decision authority.
